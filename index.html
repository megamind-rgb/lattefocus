The offer’s terms

Will it work on my Mac?+

lattefocus is a universal app for both Apple silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 13 or later. The demo on this page works in any modern browser.

Does my focus history leave my Mac?+

No. lattefocus keeps your focus history and settings on your Mac, and needs no account. It reads which app and browser tab are in front only during a focus session.

It goes online only for updates: every 6 hours it asks GitHub whether there’s a new version, and it downloads one when you choose to install it. You can turn the checks off with lattefocus → Check for Updates Automatically. This website’s demo is separate: it uses sample activity and runs only in your browser.

If you join the launch list, we keep your email address only for the launch email, and delete it at the latest 6 months after. Privacy has the details.

Can I change the session length and appearance?+

Choose a 30, 45, or 90-minute session, or set your own duration from 5 minutes to 6 hours. The moon and sun button switches lattefocus between its light and dark appearances. Try both in the preview above.

I already have lattefocus. What now?+

Keep using it: from version 0.3.0 on, the free preview updates itself, as before. If yours is older, email us and we’ll help you move to the newest version.
